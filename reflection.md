## My reflection
Examining the water flow records on November 18 proved that there was a strong correlation between time of day, water pressure, and customer waiting times. Even though the pump worked effectively throughout the whole process, the performance of the system varied greatly depending on demand. In the off-peak hours of the early afternoon and evening, the water flow was generally high, resulting in short queues of one to four customers and waiting times less than six minutes. In contrast, in the evening rush hour of 16:40 to 19:30, the queue was as long as eight customers, and the water flow rate was only moderate and low.
